Where to See Both Collections in MongoDB Compass

  1. Open the MongoDB Compass window on your computer.
  2. Connect using: mongodb://localhost:27017
  3. In the left sidebar, click the database named skillplay_db.
  4. You will see two collections:
    • users: Stores all registered users (Name, Email, Mobile No, Password hash, Points).
    • loginhistories: Stores every login event each time someone signs in!
  5. Click either collection and hit the Refresh (⟳) button to see your live data!
